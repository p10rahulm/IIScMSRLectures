<b>Abstract:</b>
Given two matroids <math id="abstract1.m1" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\matroid_{1}" display="inline" intent=":literal"><semantics><msub><mi class="ltx_font_mathcaligraphic">ℳ</mi><mn>1</mn></msub><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\matroid_{1}</annotation></semantics></math> and <math id="abstract1.m2" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\matroid_{2}" display="inline" intent=":literal"><semantics><msub><mi class="ltx_font_mathcaligraphic">ℳ</mi><mn>2</mn></msub><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\matroid_{2}</annotation></semantics></math> over the same ground set, the <em id="abstract1.1.1" class="ltx_emph ltx_font_italic">matroid intersection</em> problem is to find the maximum cardinality common independent set. In the weighted version of the problem, the goal is to find a maximum weight common independent set. It has been a matter of interest to find efficient approximation algorithms for this problem in various settings. In many of these models, there is a gap between the best known results for the unweighted and weighted versions. In this work, we address the question of closing this gap. Our main result is a <em id="abstract1.1.2" class="ltx_emph ltx_font_italic">reduction</em> which converts any <math id="abstract1.m3" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\alpha" display="inline" intent=":literal"><semantics><mi>α</mi><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\alpha</annotation></semantics></math>-approximate unweighted matroid intersection algorithm into an <math id="abstract1.m4" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\alpha(1-\varepsilon)" display="inline" intent=":literal"><semantics><mrow><mi>α</mi><mo>⁡</mo><mrow><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><mrow><mn>1</mn><mo>−</mo><mi>ε</mi></mrow><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\alpha(1-\varepsilon)</annotation></semantics></math>-approximate weighted matroid intersection algorithm, while increasing the runtime of the algorithm by a <math id="abstract1.m5" class="ltx_Math" alttext="\log W" display="inline" intent=":literal"><semantics><mrow><mi>log</mi><mo lspace="0.167em">⁡</mo><mi>W</mi></mrow><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">\log W</annotation></semantics></math> factor, where <math id="abstract1.m6" class="ltx_Math" alttext="W" display="inline" intent=":literal"><semantics><mi>W</mi><annotation encoding="application/x-tex">W</annotation></semantics></math> is the aspect ratio. Our framework is versatile and translates to settings such as streaming and one-way communication complexity where matroid intersection is well-studied. As a by-product of our techniques, we derive new results for weighted matroid intersection in these models.
